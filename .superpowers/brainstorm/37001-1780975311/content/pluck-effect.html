Pluck Effect

When a finger crosses a string and triggers a note — what does it look like?

A — White Flash

Triggered string briefly blazes white and oversized, then fades back to its hue color. Sharp, percussive — matches the harp pluck sound.

B — Ripple to Neighbors

Plucking one string sends energy to the strings on either side, like a real curtain of strings. Creates fluid, organic wave motion through the field.

C — Glow Pulse

Plucked string expands into a large soft bloom that slowly decays over ~1s. No white flash — just a deep, sustained glow burst. Dreamy, atmospheric.
