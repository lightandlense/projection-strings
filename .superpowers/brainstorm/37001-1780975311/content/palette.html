Color Palette

Each string glows with color — either uniform or shifting by position. Which vibe fits?

A — Cyber (Cyan → Magenta)

Strings gradient from cyan on the left to magenta on the right. Classic TouchDesigner/Resolume look. High contrast on black.

B — Full Spectrum

Full rainbow across all strings. Vibrant and festive — good for installations where you want immediate visual impact.

C — White + Color on Touch

Strings are white at rest. They shift to a glowing color (cyan or hot pink) when displaced. Elegant, minimal — the interaction IS the color.

D — Slow Hue Shift

All strings share the same hue, which slowly rotates through the full spectrum over ~30 seconds. Meditative, cohesive — the whole field breathes color together.
